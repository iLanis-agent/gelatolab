GelatoLab

Every home gelato maker hits the same wall: the recipe freezes into a brick, or never sets at all. The cause is that dextrose, honey and glucose syrup fight ice crystals far better than table sugar per gram, and nobody does that math at the counter. GelatoLab does it for your exact mix.

Balance my mix
  • Full mix readout: fat, dairy solids, added sugars, total solids and batch yield with overrun.
  • Sweetness (POD) and anti-freeze power (PAC) from standard published sugar tables.
  • Frozen-water percentage at your serving temperature - the number that decides scoopable versus brick - from the cryoscopic constant, not folklore.
  • A fix solver: name your target sweetness and hardness, get the exact grams of sucrose and dextrose to add or remove.
  • Works for sorbet too (water or juice base). Runs entirely in your browser.
Constants are standard published values and conventions vary between sources; the freezing curve is an ideal-dilute physical model. Test a new mix with a small batch.
