Rock-hard gelato is not bad luck, it is sugar chemistry. Different sugars sweeten differently and freeze differently - dextrose depresses freezing 1.7x more than sucrose per gram. Balance the mix with physics, then scoop it.

Composition and sweetness constants are standard published values (Corvitto-style tables; conventions vary by source). The freezing curve is the ideal-dilute physical model: honest physics, not a fit to your freezer. Verify a new mix with a small batch.
